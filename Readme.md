# PostalNook website: what changed and why it helps

A short summary. "Expected impact" is our best judgement. Check it with real data after launch (see the last section).

## 1. Easier to explore services
- **Services menu:** one wide menu with three short groups (Mailboxes, Online & Address, Your U.S. Business) and a "View all services" bar. It is shorter, easier to scan and easier to click.
  *Expected impact: visitors find the right service in fewer clicks.*
- **Service cards on the home page:** each card has a short hook and one or two clear lines. Every detail is kept. The whole card is clickable and opens the matching page inside the site.
  *Expected impact: less reading, more clicks to service pages.*
- **Pages are intentionally a little longer.** We added space between sections and gave each service its own card and block. Each section now holds one idea. Visitors scroll through the services one by one instead of facing a dense wall of text.
  *Expected impact: services are easier to see, compare and remember.*

## 2. One message across the whole site
- **One customer count** ("over {{customers}} satisfied customers") is used everywhere. Change it in one place.
- **One main button.** It says "Get Started" on every page. The second button says "Learn more".
- **One type scale.** Headings, body text, cards and buttons use the same sizes on every page.
- **One H1 per page.** This helps search engines and screen readers.
- **One terms line.** Forms now say only "I agree to the Terms and Conditions".
- **Same header, footer, menu and look** on every page, including the 404 page.
  *Expected impact: the brand feels more trustworthy and professional. Search engines read each page more clearly.*

## 3. U.S. LLC Formation
- **One entry page** at `/us-llc-formation`. Visitors from India or Pakistan go straight to their own page. Everyone else picks a country. The country pages still work with their own links.
- **Redesigned pages:** the offer and the $799 price come first. Then what is included, what is not included, how it works, who it is for, and FAQs. Being clear about limits builds trust.
  *Expected impact: fewer confused visitors and better quality leads.*

## 4. A 404 page that keeps visitors
- The page is part of the project, so it is easy to edit. It has the full header and footer.
- It shows **all services as small cards** and **a blog list with type-to-search**. It also has clear buttons to the homepage and to Contact.
- *Expected impact: a visitor who lands on a wrong link finds something useful and stays on the site.*

## 5. Search and sharing
- Every page has a title, a description, a canonical link, social-share tags and structured data (business details, FAQs).
- The package also includes a sitemap, `robots.txt`, a site icon and a share image.
  *Expected impact: better search results and better link previews when people share the site.*

## 6. Smooth and safe to use
- Smooth scrolling and gentle reveals on large screens. Phones use normal scrolling.
- A progress bar sits at the bottom and rests on the footer. The open menu stays above it.
- The layout was built phone first. Logos and images are the real ones.
- The package opens from a hosting service **and** from the folder on your computer.
