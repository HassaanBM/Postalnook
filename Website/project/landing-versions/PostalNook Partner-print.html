The Business

What is PostalNook?

A modern alternative to the PO Box. Each PostalNook location gives individuals and businesses a real street address, secure mailboxes, package handling, and a digital mailbox they can manage from anywhere in the world.

📦

Physical hub

Secure mailboxes, package receiving, shipping & in-store services from a retail storefront.

Digital platform

Customers view, scan, forward & shred mail online, the stickiness that drives retention.
